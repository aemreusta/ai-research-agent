---
name: market-sizing
description: Market size, market share, growth rate or forecast questions, where sources often disagree because of methodology, scope or base year.
version: 1
---
Market figures disagree more often than facts do, and usually for a reason. Before calling two
figures a contradiction, compare:
- scope: which segments, which geography (global, EU, Turkey), hardware vs. software;
- metric: revenue, spending, ARR, transaction volume, installed base;
- base year and whether the figure is actual or a forecast (and forecast horizon);
- currency and whether it is nominal or inflation-adjusted;
- methodology: top-down analyst estimate, bottom-up survey, company filings.

Prefer primary research firms' own summaries (their press releases or report abstracts) over
articles quoting them; quotes often drop the scope or the year. Always report a figure with its
year, scope and source; when figures conflict, report the range and explain the difference
rather than averaging them. CAGR claims need a start year, end year and the two endpoints.
