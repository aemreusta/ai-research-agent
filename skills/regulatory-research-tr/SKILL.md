Work from the hierarchy of Turkish legal sources, highest first:
1. Laws (Kanun) and presidential decrees as published in the Resmî Gazete (resmigazete.gov.tr)
   and consolidated on mevzuat.gov.tr. Cite the law number (e.g. "6698 sayılı KVKK").
2. Regulations (Yönetmelik) and communiqués (Tebliğ) of the competent authority.
3. Regulator decisions and guidance: for data protection the KVKK Board decisions (Kurul
   Kararları), guides (Rehber) and announcements on kvkk.gov.tr; for e-commerce and consumer
   law ticaret.gov.tr; for electronic communications btk.gov.tr.
4. Court decisions (Danıştay, Yargıtay, Anayasa Mahkemesi).
5. Law-firm briefings and news - useful for orientation, never the final word on a date or an
   amount.

Dates: distinguish publication date (yayım tarihi) from entry into force (yürürlük tarihi) and
from the end of a transition period (geçiş süresi). Many "2026 obligations" are older rules
whose transition period ends in 2026 - say which of these a finding is.

Amounts: administrative fines (idari para cezası) are revalued every year; always state the year
the amount applies to.

For action plans: every recommended action must trace back to a specific obligation found in a
primary source (the article, the Board decision or the guide), and actions should be ordered by
deadline. KVKK 2024 amendments changed cross-border transfer rules (standard contracts, binding
corporate rules, notification within five business days) - check whether a source predates them.
