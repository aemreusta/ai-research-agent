name: company-research
description: Questions about a specific company or startup - products, partnerships, funding, leadership, strategy - especially small or regional companies with thin press coverage.
version: 1
---
Primary sources for a company, in order: its own website (product pages, "about", press or
news room, blog), its official social accounts, investor or funding announcements it
published, trade-registry and official gazette entries (Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi for Turkish
companies), then partners' own announcements. Coverage by news sites and startup databases is
secondary.

Syndication trap: small companies' press releases are republished word for word by many sites.
Five articles with the same text are one source. Prefer the company's own release and treat the
copies as corroborating nothing.

For partnerships, a partnership is established only if at least one party announced it; a
logo on a slide or a "we work with" list is weaker evidence - say so. For funding, state the
round, amount, currency, date and lead investor only as sources state them.

Thin coverage is itself a finding: if a sub-question cannot be answered from public sources,
it belongs in Known Gaps, not in guesses. Watch for companies with similar names - check the
domain, country and founders before attributing a fact.
