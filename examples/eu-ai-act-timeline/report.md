# Changes to the EU AI Act Implementation Timeline

## Summary

The implementation timeline for the EU AI Act was updated to account for amendments introduced by the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744), which was adopted on 29 June 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026. _(single source)_ [1][2] The compliance deadline for Annex III stand-alone high-risk AI systems was postponed from the original application date of 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027. [3][4][5] Compliance obligations for Annex I high-risk AI systems embedded into regulated products were postponed to 2 August 2028, while the deadline for establishing national AI regulatory sandboxes was postponed to 2 August 2027. [3][5][6] The Digital Omnibus also introduced a new prohibition covering AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material, applying from 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [3][6][7]

## Key Findings

- The Council of the EU formally adopted the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) on 29 June 2026, amending the implementation timeline upon entering into force on 27 July 2026. _(single source)_ [1][2]
- The compliance deadline for Annex III high-risk AI systems was postponed from the original date of 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027. [3][4][5]
- The compliance deadline for Annex I high-risk AI systems embedded in regulated products was postponed to 2 August 2028. _(single source)_ [1][5][6]
- The deadline for Member States to ensure at least one operational AI regulatory sandbox was postponed to 2 August 2027. [1][3][5]
- A ninth prohibition targeting AI-generated non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material comes into effect on 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [3][6][7]
- The AI Omnibus postponed transparency obligations for AI-generated synthetic content to 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [5][8]
- Enforcement of the AI Act, including prohibited practices and transparency rules, began on 2 August 2026. _(single source)_ [3][6][9]
