# Changes to the EU AI Act Implementation Timeline

## Summary

The EU AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024, with general prohibitions taking effect on 2 February 2025 and obligations for general-purpose AI models applying from 2 August 2025. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8] Although the original deadline for high-risk AI rules was 2 August 2026, the timeline for enforcement was extended by the AI Omnibus to 2 December 2027 for stand-alone high-risk systems and 2 August 2028 for high-risk AI embedded in products. _(single source)_ [1][9][10] In addition, the deadline for providers to implement transparency solutions for artificially generated content was set to 2 December 2026, and a prohibition on non-consensual intimate content takes effect in December 2026. _(single source)_ [1][7][10][11]

## Key Findings

- The EU AI Act officially entered into force on 1 August 2024, and prohibitions on prohibited AI practices took effect on 2 February 2025. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7]
- Obligations and rules for providers of general-purpose AI models became applicable on 2 August 2025. _(single source)_ [2][8]
- The application deadline for stand-alone high-risk AI systems was delayed from the original date of 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027. _(single source)_ [1][7][9][10]
- The deadline for the enforcement of high-risk AI systems embedded in products was extended to 2 August 2028. _(single source)_ [1][7][9][10]
- The grace period for providers to implement transparency solutions for artificially generated content was adjusted to a deadline of 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [7][10]
- A prohibition on AI systems generating or manipulating non-consensual intimate content comes into effect on 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [1][11]
- The deadline for national competent authorities to establish AI regulatory sandboxes was postponed to 2 August 2027. _(single source)_ [10]
