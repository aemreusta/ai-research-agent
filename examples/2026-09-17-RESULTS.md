- The first KVKK repeat includes a real SIGKILL/recovery. Its 306-second observed duration is correct; the 219-second DB duration predates the started-time preservation fix.
- The Pro PostgreSQL report predates moving final citation verification to the reasoning tier. Its monotonicity overgeneralization is covered by a later live negative/positive verifier probe.
- Legal overview pages can omit exceptions. The post-policy EU report preserves a narrow legacy-system qualifier but loses useful deadline coverage. The KVKK report still misinterprets a notification-role choice; further expert semantic review is needed.
- Full states are `state.json.gz`, compressed losslessly and checked byte-for-byte. Use `gzip -dc path/to/state.json.gz` to inspect; the export-audit script reads them directly. Raw source text was scanned before compression; reviewed scanner findings were public document/community IDs, an anonymous video redirect token or a SQL example.
