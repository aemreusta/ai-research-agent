# EU AI Act Implementation Timeline Changes

## Summary

The EU AI Act's high-risk application dates were formally extended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, which entered into force on 27 July 2026. [1][2] The stated reason for extending the high-risk application deadlines was the readiness of supporting infrastructure, specifically the lack of harmonised standards and an insufficient number of designated notified bodies. [1][3] Earlier implementation milestones remained in place, including general provisions and prohibitions applying from 2 February 2025, General-Purpose AI rules applying from 2 August 2025, and general applicability taking effect on 2 August 2026. [4][5][6][7][8][9][10]

## Key Findings

- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 formally extended the high-risk application dates under the EU AI Act and entered into force on 27 July 2026. [1][2]
- The readiness of supporting infrastructure, specifically the absence of harmonised standards and an insufficient number of designated notified bodies, was the stated reason for extending the high-risk deadlines. [1][3]
- According to a single source, the compliance deadline for Annex III high-risk AI systems was extended from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027. _(single source)_ [1]
- According to a single source, the compliance deadline for Annex I product-safety high-risk AI systems was extended from 2 August 2027 to 2 August 2028. _(single source)_ [1]
- According to a single source, prohibitions related to the generation or manipulation of non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material apply from 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [11]
- The statutory applicability date for GPAI rules remained 2 August 2025, with the final iteration of the GPAI Code of Practice released on 10 July 2025 after initially being expected by 2 May 2025 according to a single source. [4][5][6][12]
- The EU AI Act generally became applicable on 2 August 2026. [6][7][8][9][10]

## Conclusion

The primary timeline changes to the EU AI Act were the formal postponements enacted by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 due to infrastructure readiness constraints. [1][2][3] Single sources report that compliance dates for Annex III and Annex I high-risk systems were shifted to 2 December 2027 and 2 August 2028 respectively, while prohibitions, GPAI requirements, and general applicability took effect as originally scheduled. [1][4][5][6][7][8]
