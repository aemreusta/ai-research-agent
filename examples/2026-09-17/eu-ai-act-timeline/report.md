# Changes to the EU AI Act Implementation Timeline

## Summary

The EU AI Act officially entered into force on 1 August 2024. [1][2][3][4][5] According to a single source, the AI Omnibus entered into force across the EU on 27 July 2026, introducing targeted simplifications and extended compliance timelines. _(single source)_ [6] A single source reports that compliance deadlines were deferred from August 2, 2026, to December 2, 2027, for Annex III standalone high-risk AI systems, and from August 2, 2027, to August 2, 2028, for Annex I high-risk systems. _(single source)_ [7]

## Key Findings

- According to a single source, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was adopted on 8 July 2026 and published on 24 July 2026 to amend Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 regarding the simplification of implementation rules. _(single source)_ [8]
- A single source states that the compliance deadline for Annex III standalone high-risk systems was deferred from August 2, 2026, to December 2, 2027. _(single source)_ [7]
- A single source indicates that the compliance deadline for Annex I high-risk AI systems embedded in regulated products was deferred from August 2, 2027, to August 2, 2028. _(single source)_ [7]
- According to a single source, watermarking obligations under Article 50(2) for systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 were postponed to 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [9][10]
- A single source reports that a new prohibition on AI systems generating non-consensual sexually explicit content or child sexual abuse material takes effect in December 2026. _(single source)_ [5]
- CEN/CENELEC harmonised standards have experienced delays, with the first standards potentially not reaching publication until Q4 2026. [11][12][13]
- European Standard EN 18286:2026 concerning quality management systems for EU AI Act regulatory purposes has been published. [14][15]

## Conflicting / Uncertain Information

- One contested finding indicates that the Commission's Digital Omnibus proposal of November 19, 2025, proposed extending the Annex III high-risk AI deadline to December 2, 2027. [11]
- In contrast, another contested finding reports that the Digital Omnibus proposal included a delay of the Annex I high-risk AI system deadline to December 2, 2028. [11]

## Conclusion

According to single-source reporting, the AI Omnibus enacted in July 2026 extended key high-risk compliance timelines while baseline EU AI Act enforcement commenced on 2 August 2026. _(single source, approximate)_ [5][6] Although EN 18286:2026 has been published, some questions remain open regarding the timing of remaining harmonised standards delayed into late 2026.
