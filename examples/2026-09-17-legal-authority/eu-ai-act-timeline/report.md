## Key Findings

- According to a single source, the GPAI Code of Practice was initially expected by 2 May 2025, but its final version was published on 10 July 2025, more than two months later than initially planned. [4][5][10]
- The EU AI Act rules regarding General-Purpose AI became applicable starting from 2 August 2025. _(single source)_ [3][10]
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was published on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026 to simplify the implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence. [6][7][11][12]
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 changed the timeline for Article 6(2)/Annex III and Article 6(1)/Annex I high-risk routes, moving them to later dates. [6][7]
- A single source reports that a narrow Article 50(2) legacy-system transition ends on 2 December 2026. _(single source)_ [6]

## Conflicting / Uncertain Information

## Conclusion

While GPAI rules took effect on 2 August 2025 following the release of the GPAI Code of Practice on 10 July 2025, the high-risk system timelines under Annex I and Annex III were postponed by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. [3][4][5][6][7] The general rules of the AI Act became applicable on 2 August 2026, with specific transition periods and delegated act deadlines continuing into December 2026 and August 2027. _(single source, approximate)_ [6][8][9][16]
